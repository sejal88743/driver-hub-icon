Advanced Multi-Bill Split & FIFO Allocation AlgorithmWhen a single physical cheque covers multiple bills, the system must split the Cheque Amount sequentially across the rows based on the Outstanding Amount.Allocation Rules:Exact Matching: Read the total Cheque Amount for that unique Cheque Number.Sequential Deductions:Row 1 (Bill A): Collected Amount = Minimum of (Outstanding Amount, Remaining Cheque Amount).Remaining Cheque Balance: Subtract the allocated amount from the total Cheque Amount.Row 2 (Bill B): If Cheque Balance > 0, apply it to the next bill's outstanding amount.
